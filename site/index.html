ليالي · nights

A small room with a big welcome.

Layali means "nights" in Arabic, and it's named for the long evenings spent with family and friends over something sweet and something warm. We wanted South C to have a place that feels the same way.

The room is filled with woven baskets, old lanterns, hand-painted plates and a kilim from home. The menu has drinks our founder grew up loving next to new favourites. Pull up, say hi, and stay a while.

— The Layali family
